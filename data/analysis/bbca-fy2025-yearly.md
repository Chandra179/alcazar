# BBCA Year by Year

Bank Central Asia (BBCA) from the Asian financial crisis through the latest reviewed 2026 disclosures.

Share-price changes compare year-end closes after adjusting for stock splits. Annual returns including dividends assume you held the shares all year. They count dividends as cash and are before taxes and trading fees. <a id="year-2026"></a>

## 2026 so far — Profit grew in both updates

**At the bank.** Group profit growth picked up in September after a slower first half. Through September, profit for BCA and its subsidiaries rose 4.2% compared with the same months a year earlier. Through October, profit for BCA bank only rose 4.4% compared with the same months a year earlier.

**For shareholders.** The reviewed snapshot has no matching share price and dividend record for a return through October.

**Why it mattered.** The September and October releases cover different businesses and time spans. Each compares profit with its own prior-year period, so October is a separate check on the bank’s performance.

 [September 2026 group results](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Triwulan/2026/20261027-laporan-keuangan-konsolidasi-pt-bca-tbk-periode-30-september-2026-billingual.pdf#page=8) [BCA October 2026 publication](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Bulanan/2026/20261113-laporan-keuangan-publikasi-bulanan-oktober-2026-eng.pdf#page=1)

<a id="year-2025"></a>

## 2025 — BCA earned more while its shares fell

**At the bank.** BCA lent more and earned more from its operations. It also booked higher charges for potential losses, which held back profit growth. Group profit rose 4.9% to Rp57.6 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price fell 16.5%. Return with dividends was -13.4%.

**Why it mattered.** Dividends cushioned part of the loss. By year-end, investors were paying less for each rupiah of annual profit than they had a year earlier.

 [2025 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=15) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=17)

<a id="year-2024"></a>

## 2024 — More lending helped grow the business

**At the bank.** BCA lent more and brought in more income from its everyday banking business. Group profit rose 12.7% to Rp54.9 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 2.9%. Return with dividends was 5.9%.

**Why it mattered.** The shares gained much less than profit grew. Dividend payments added to the gain for shareholders who held through the year.

 [2024 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2025/20250306-BCA-AR-2024-ENG.pdf#page=20) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=17)

<a id="year-2023"></a>

## 2023 — Interest income and lower loss charges lifted profit

**At the bank.** BCA earned more interest after funding costs, while the amount charged against earnings for potential losses fell. Group profit rose 19.4% to Rp48.7 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 9.9%. Return with dividends was 12.4%.

**Why it mattered.** A bigger loan book and lower loss charges helped more of the bank's income reach the bottom line. Shareholders also saw a gain in the share price and received dividends.

 [2023 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2024/20240215-buku-bca-ar-2023-eng-website.pdf#page=18) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=17)

<a id="year-2022"></a>

## 2022 — Profit climbed as loss charges eased

**At the bank.** Interest income after funding costs rose. Charges for potential losses fell sharply from the previous year. Group profit rose 29.6% to Rp40.8 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 17.1%. Return with dividends was 19.2%.

**Why it mattered.** BCA made more from banking and gave up less of that income to cover possible losses. That combination supported a strong rise in profit after the pandemic disruption.

 [2022 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20230216-bca-ar-2022-inggris.pdf#page=18) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=17)

<a id="year-2021"></a>

## 2021 — Profit recovered and BCA split its shares

**At the bank.** Customer deposits grew and charges for potential losses eased as BCA continued working with borrowers affected by the pandemic. Group profit rose 15.8% to Rp31.4 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 7.8%. Return with dividends was 9.5%.

**Why it mattered.** The October stock split gave holders five shares for each share they previously owned. Price changes here account for the split so the larger share count does not appear as an investment loss.

 [2021 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2022/20220217-buku-ar-bca-2021-EN.pdf#page=20) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=17)

<a id="year-2020"></a>

## 2020 — The pandemic interrupted profit growth

**At the bank.** BCA reworked payment terms for affected borrowers. Lending contracted on the bank's published comparison and charges for potential losses rose. Group profit fell 5.0% to Rp27.1 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 1.3%. Return with dividends was 2.3%.

**Why it mattered.** Loan relief gave borrowers more time to pay. Higher loss charges reduced current profit even as customer deposits grew. The shares ended the year slightly higher.

 [BCA 2020 results](https://www.bca.co.id/en/tentang-bca/media-riset/pressroom/siaran-pers/2021/02/19/04/08/Hasil-Kinerja-Sepanjang-Tahun-2020-Kinerja-Solid-dan-Berkelanjutan-di-Masa-Pandemi) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20210226AR-2020-BCAInggris-Medium-Res.pdf#page=20)

<a id="year-2019"></a>

## 2019 — More deposits and interest income

**At the bank.** Balances in current and savings accounts rose, and BCA earned more interest after paying its own interest costs. Group profit rose 10.5% to Rp28.6 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 28.6%. Return with dividends was 29.9%.

**Why it mattered.** Money in everyday customer accounts can be a low-cost source of funding. That helps support lending and interest income when the bank can find borrowers on suitable terms.

 [2019 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20200311-AR-BCA19-ENG.pdf#page=18) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20210226AR-2020-BCAInggris-Medium-Res.pdf#page=20)

<a id="year-2018"></a>

## 2018 — Savings and current accounts grew

**At the bank.** Customers held more money in savings and current accounts. Income from lending and other investments also rose after funding costs. Group profit rose 10.9% to Rp25.9 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 18.7%. Return with dividends was 19.9%.

**Why it mattered.** Money in those accounts helps fund the business. Growth in both deposits and net interest income gave BCA more low-cost funding to put to work and more income after paying interest.

 [2018 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20190313-ar-bca-eng.pdf#page=12) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20210226AR-2020-BCAInggris-Medium-Res.pdf#page=20)

<a id="year-2017"></a>

## 2017 — Profit outgrew net interest income

**At the bank.** The bank extended more credit and attracted more deposits. Its interest income after funding costs grew more slowly than profit. Group profit rose 13.0% to Rp23.3 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 41.3%. Return with dividends was 42.6%.

**Why it mattered.** A growing loan book can support future income. The different growth rates show why overall profit depends on more than the amount the bank earns after interest costs.

 [2017 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20180323-laporan-tahunan-2017-EN.pdf#page=26) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20210226AR-2020-BCAInggris-Medium-Res.pdf#page=20)

<a id="year-2016"></a>

## 2016 — Interest income rose despite larger loss charges

**At the bank.** BCA earned more interest after funding costs and attracted more customer deposits. Charges for potential losses also increased. Group profit rose 14.4% to Rp20.6 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 16.5%. Return with dividends was 17.9%.

**Why it mattered.** Higher income helped the bank absorb the extra charges and still earn more. The year illustrates how possible loan losses can take a bite out of the benefits of growth.

 [2016 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20170315-buku-bca-ar16-EN.pdf#page=14) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/20210226AR-2020-BCAInggris-Medium-Res.pdf#page=20)

<a id="year-2015"></a>

## 2015 — Profit grew while the share price barely moved

**At the bank.** Interest income after funding costs increased, but charges for potential losses also grew. Group profit rose 9.2% to Rp18.0 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 1.3%. Return with dividends was 2.5%.

**Why it mattered.** The bank finished the year with more profit, while the share price ended near where it started. Cash dividends made up a substantial share of a holder's gain.

 [2015 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/BCA-AR-2015-Eng.pdf#page=24) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/BCA-AR-2015-Eng.pdf#page=28)

<a id="year-2014"></a>

## 2014 — Interest income and deposits rose

**At the bank.** BCA attracted more customer deposits and earned more interest after paying its funding costs. Group profit rose 15.8% to Rp16.5 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 36.7%. Return with dividends was 38.0%.

**Why it mattered.** The bank had more funding to work with, and more income remained after paying interest. Shareholders saw a large price gain alongside the improvement in annual profit.

 [2014 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/annual_report2014_eng.pdf#page=20) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/BCA-AR-2015-Eng.pdf#page=28)

<a id="year-2013"></a>

## 2013 — The bank kept more of its interest income

**At the bank.** BCA's income after funding costs climbed. Customer deposits also increased over the year. Group profit rose 21.7% to Rp14.3 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 5.5%. Return with dividends was 6.8%.

**Why it mattered.** For a bank, the difference between interest earned and interest paid is a major source of profit. BCA's profit rose strongly even though the share price moved by much less.

 [2013 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2013.pdf#page=22) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2013.pdf#page=26)

<a id="year-2012"></a>

## 2012 — Lending grew and the share of problem loans fell

**At the bank.** The loan book expanded faster than customer deposits. The bank's reported share of loans in trouble fell. Group profit rose 8.3% to Rp11.7 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 13.8%. Return with dividends was 15.2%.

**Why it mattered.** Lending can lift income, but growing loans faster than deposits makes the funding mix worth watching. Reported loan trouble was low at year-end.

 [2012 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2012.pdf#page=18) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2013.pdf#page=26)

<a id="year-2011"></a>

## 2011 — Lending expanded and profit climbed

**At the bank.** The loan book grew faster than customer deposits. The bank reported a slightly smaller share of problem loans at year-end. Group profit rose 27.6% to Rp10.8 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 25.0%. Return with dividends was 26.8%.

**Why it mattered.** More lending gave BCA more opportunities to earn interest. Deposit growth helped finance that expansion, while the share of problem loans gave readers a way to judge its early risks.

 [2011 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2011.pdf#page=12) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2013.pdf#page=26)

<a id="year-2010"></a>

## 2010 — Loan growth picked up

**At the bank.** BCA expanded lending faster than its customer deposits grew. The bank reported a slightly lower share of problem loans. Group profit rose 24.6% to Rp8.5 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 32.0%. Return with dividends was 34.3%.

**Why it mattered.** The loan book was becoming a bigger part of how BCA put customer deposits to work. That creates more room to earn interest and more exposure to whether borrowers repay.

 [2010 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2010.pdf#page=12) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2013.pdf#page=26)

<a id="year-2009"></a>

## 2009 — Deposits grew faster than lending

**At the bank.** After the global financial crisis, BCA attracted more customer deposits. Loans and interest income after funding costs also rose. Group profit rose 17.9% to Rp6.8 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 49.2%. Return with dividends was 53.5%.

**Why it mattered.** The bank gained more customer funding than it used for additional loans. Shareholders had a strong positive year following the prior year's negative return.

 [2009 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2009.pdf#page=6) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2009.pdf#page=9)

<a id="year-2008"></a>

## 2008 — The bank grew through a difficult market

**At the bank.** During the global financial crisis, BCA's loan book expanded and its reported share of problem loans fell. The bank also split each share into two. Group profit rose 28.7% to Rp5.8 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price fell 11.0%. Return with dividends was -9.2%.

**Why it mattered.** Better annual results did not prevent a market loss. Holders ended the year down even after dividends, with share prices adjusted for the split.

 [2008 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2008.pdf#page=12) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2008.pdf#page=15)

<a id="year-2007"></a>

## 2007 — Lending and customer deposits both expanded

**At the bank.** BCA's loan book grew faster than its customer deposits. The bank reported a smaller share of problem loans. Group profit rose 5.8% to Rp4.5 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 40.4%. Return with dividends was 43.7%.

**Why it mattered.** More lending gave the bank room to earn additional interest. The reported improvement in loan quality was encouraging as the amount outstanding expanded.

 [2008 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2008.pdf#page=12) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2008.pdf#page=15)

<a id="year-2006"></a>

## 2006 — Customer deposits recovered

**At the bank.** Customer deposits rose after falling the year before. BCA's loan book and interest income after funding costs also grew. Group profit rose 17.9% to Rp4.2 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 52.9%. Return with dividends was 57.2%.

**Why it mattered.** More customer funding supported further lending. The bank also reported a smaller share of problem loans than it had a year earlier.

 [2006 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2006.pdf#page=11) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2006.pdf#page=14)

<a id="year-2005"></a>

## 2005 — More lending came with more signs of strain

**At the bank.** The bank lent more and earned more interest after funding costs. The share of problem loans edged up from the previous year. Group profit rose 12.6% to Rp3.6 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 14.3%. Return with dividends was 18.7%.

**Why it mattered.** Higher lending helped grow the business, while the increase in problem loans illustrated the need to watch who can repay. The shares still ended the year higher.

 [2005 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2005.pdf#page=4) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2005.pdf#page=7)

<a id="year-2004"></a>

## 2004 — The lending recovery gathered pace

**At the bank.** BCA lent more, earned more net interest and reported a smaller share of problem loans. It also carried out a two-for-one split in June. Group profit rose 33.7% to Rp3.2 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 78.9%. Return with dividends was 88.7%.

**Why it mattered.** The business earned more on a larger lending base. The large share-price gain shown here accounts for the split, which by itself only changed the number of shares each investor held.

 [2004 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2004.pdf#page=4) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2004.pdf#page=7)

<a id="year-2003"></a>

## 2003 — Lending expanded while profit slipped

**At the bank.** The bank made more loans and attracted more deposits, but income after funding costs declined slightly. Group profit fell 5.9% to Rp2.4 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 33.0%. Return with dividends was 42.0%.

**Why it mattered.** Growing the loan book did not translate into higher profit that year. Shareholders saw the opposite direction in the market, where the share price rose and cash dividends added to the return.

 [2003 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2003.pdf#page=1) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2003.pdf#page=4)

<a id="year-2002"></a>

## 2002 — The bank grew as a tax charge weighed on profit

**At the bank.** BCA expanded lending and attracted more deposits. The annual report explains that a charge for deferred tax reduced reported profit. Group profit fell 18.5% to Rp2.5 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 69.5%. Return with dividends was 79.0%.

**Why it mattered.** The tax charge reduced accounting profit without taking the same amount out of cash that year. The shares rose despite the decline in reported profit.

 [2002 annual report](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2002.pdf#page=89) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2002.pdf#page=75)

<a id="year-2001"></a>

## 2001 — The bank lent more in its first full year on the exchange

**At the bank.** BCA expanded lending and earned more interest after funding costs. The bank split its shares two for one in May. Group profit was Rp3.1 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The share price rose 76.1%. Return with dividends was 86.3%.

**Why it mattered.** The year marked a step toward normal banking after restructuring. It also provides the first full calendar year in this report for comparing the share move with the return including dividends.

 [BCA annual report FY2002](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2002.pdf#page=2) [Share-price records](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/AR-2002.pdf#page=75)

<a id="year-2000"></a>

## 2000 — BBCA began trading publicly

**At the bank.** BCA regained normal bank supervision and listed publicly in May. It also reorganized its accounts in October to start with a balance sheet that reflected the restructuring. Group profit was Rp1.8 trillion.

**For shareholders.** The listing year. BBCA began trading in May, so there is no full-calendar return. The verified year-end price was Rp41.875 on the split-adjusted scale.

**Why it mattered.** Investors could now buy the shares on the exchange. The October accounting change is important when comparing this year's results and balances with the years around it.

 [Audited 2000 statements](https://studylibid.com/doc/1976703/pt-bank-central-asia-tbk-dan-anak-perusahaan) [Share-price records](https://repository.unas.ac.id/id/eprint/2309/1/EKA%20WIDIA.pdf)

<a id="year-1999"></a>

## 1999 — Government support restored positive equity

**At the bank.** The government recapitalized BCA, restoring positive year-end equity after the prior year's deficit. BCA returned to profit, earning Rp0.6 trillion.

**For shareholders.** Before listing. BBCA shares did not yet trade on the exchange.

**Why it mattered.** The fresh capital helped rebuild the bank's ability to absorb losses. This was a recapitalization year, so a conventional return on average equity would be misleading.

 [BCA audited FY1999 and FY2000 split periods (Global Reports mirror)](https://studylibid.com/doc/1976703/pt-bank-central-asia-tbk-dan-anak-perusahaan) [BCA milestones](https://www.bca.co.id/en/tentang-bca/korporasi/sejarah-bca)

<a id="year-1998"></a>

## 1998 — A rush of withdrawals led to a takeover

**At the bank.** Customers rushed to withdraw money during the Asian financial crisis. A government agency took control and put the bank into a restructuring program. The group lost Rp28.4 trillion.

**For shareholders.** Before listing. BBCA shares did not yet trade on the exchange.

**Why it mattered.** The losses left BCA with negative equity, meaning the bank's recorded obligations and other claims exceeded its assets. Restoring the bank's ability to absorb losses became the priority.
