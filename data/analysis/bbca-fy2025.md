## FY2025 earnings and funding

Consolidated assets grew 9.5%, pretax income grew 4.5%, and net income grew 4.9%. The pretax increase of IDR 3.04T reconciles to IDR 3.28T more net interest and sharia income and IDR 3.38T more other operating income, offset by IDR 1.21T lower net insurance income, IDR 1.98T higher impairment expense, and IDR 0.43T higher other operating expenses. Consolidated loans grew 7.7%, corporate loans drove the largest increase, and bank-only NIM slipped 0.1 percentage point. CASA gained 2.2 percentage points of consolidated deposits. Stronger deposit mix and lower headline NPL coincided with materially higher impairment charges. ## FY2025 cash and capital

Operating cash rose from IDR 53.82T to 77.51T. Relative to 2024, customer deposit cash flows contributed IDR 83.72T more and loan cash outflows were IDR 36.06T smaller. The reverse repo line moved IDR 95.48T in the other direction. These are the largest drivers of the operating cash move. The combined activity subtotal plus FX moved cash from IDR 85.48T to 87.55T in 2025. Funding includes temporary syirkah deposits, which are shown separately from liabilities in the audited balance sheet. Including them explains why the annual report’s leverage is higher than the YAML ratio. ## Shareholder results and valuation

BBCA’s closing price fell from IDR 9,675 in 2024 to IDR 8,075 in 2025 while rounded basic EPS rose from IDR 445 to 467. The year-end price fell about 16.5% before dividends. BCA’s annual report lists FY2025 P/B of 3.8× and book value of IDR 2,288 per share. Recomputing with the audited equity and closing shares excluding treasury gives about 3.53×. The published multiple does not reconcile to the stated price and book value. Calendar 2025 paid dividends were IDR 305 per share. The separately reported FY2025 payout was IDR 336 per share, including the IDR 281 final dividend paid in April 2026. ## Dividend valuation assumptions

The five-year dividend discount scenarios use FY2025 dividend per share as the starting point. Growth for years 1–5 is 3%, 6%, or 9%. Terminal growth is 2%, 3%, or 4% and cost of equity is 12%, 11%, or 10%, respectively. Model inputs are assumptions. The value uses the FY2025 payout confirmed in March 2026, so the comparison to the FY2025 closing price is retrospective. The scenarios hold dividend growth exogenously. They do not forecast payout restrictions or model capital, funding, credit costs, and retained earnings jointly. ## September 2026 versus September 2025

Consolidated closing assets increased 6.7% year over year. Nine-month consolidated profit increased 4.2%, from IDR 43.41T to 45.24T. Closing gross loans and financing reached about IDR 1,063T, up 12.6% over September 2025, and corporate loans reached IDR 528.1T. BCA reported September loan at risk of 4.6% and NPL of 1.8%. The October bank-only balance sheet is shown separately.
