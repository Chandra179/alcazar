# BBCA fundamental analysis · FY2025

Audited FY2025 with reviewed interim disclosures through October 2026.

<a id="what-changed"></a>

## What changed

FY2025 profits grew as net interest and fee income increased. Impairment costs rose even as the headline nonperforming-loan ratio improved. Assets grew faster than earnings and returns eased. [BCA audited FY2025 · PDF 9–10](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Diaudit/2026/20260127-audited-financial-statement-bca-31-december-2025-english-combined.pdf#page=9) [BCA FY2025 annual report · PDF 16](https://www.bca.co.id/-/media/Feature/Report/File/S8/Laporan-Tahunan/2026/20260212-BCA-AR-2025-EN.pdf#page=16)

September 2026 year-to-date profit growth accelerated from the June comparison. The consolidated capital and liquidity ratios were below their FY2025 levels. The October publication covers the bank only.
